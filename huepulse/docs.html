HuePulse Technical Documentation

HuePulse is a high-performance, sound-reactive lighting system built for Philips Hue smart ecosystems. It captures acoustic audio locally, performs real-time volume/BPM analysis, and blasts binary streaming datagrams to the Hue Bridge via UDP port 2100.

Zero Cloud Latency: Audio is sampled and computed entirely in device RAM. No audio packets or voice streams are ever transmitted over the Internet or stored on disk.

1. Architecture Overview

HuePulse employs a hybrid multi-layer architecture designed for microsecond response times:

  • Audio Capture Engine (Dart/Android): 16-bit PCM streaming at 22,050 Hz via AndroidRecordConfig with unprocessed audio source, preventing audio ducking or interference when music plays on the phone (e.g., Spotify).
  • Native UDP DTLS 1.2 PSK (Kotlin/BouncyCastle): Direct socket connection on port 2100 using TLS_PSK_WITH_AES_128_GCM_SHA256 with ProtocolVersion.DTLSv12.only().
  • CLIP v2 Spatial Resolution: Automatic query of /clip/v2/resource/entertainment_configuration mapping 3D Cartesian coordinates (X, Y, Z) to entertainment channel IDs.
  • Safety Governor (REST Fallback): In case UDP is disabled or unreachable, a token-bucket throttled coalescing queue dispatches Zigbee broadcasts while enforcing the strict physical 10 req/s threshold on Hue Bridge v2.

2. Quick Setup & Network Requirements

  1. Create an Entertainment Area: Open the official Philips Hue app on iOS/Android, go to Settings → Entertainment Areas, and select your room with at least 1 color lamp or lightstrip.
  2. Same Local Wi-Fi Subnet: Connect your smartphone to the same Wi-Fi network as your Philips Hue Bridge (2.4 GHz or 5 GHz). Ensure AP Client Isolation is disabled in your router.
  3. Launch HuePulse: The app will auto-discover your Bridge via mDNS and Philips N-UPnP. Press the large physical circular button on your Hue Bridge once when prompted to authenticate.

3. HueStream Binary Protocol (v2.0)

Streaming datagrams adhere to the Hue Entertainment v2 binary layout:

Header (16 Bytes):
  [0..8]   "HueStream" (ASCII)
  [9..10]  0x02, 0x00 (Protocol Version 2.0)
  [11]     0x00 (Sequence Counter)
  [12..13] 0x00, 0x00 (Reserved)
  [14]     0x00 (Color Space: RGB 16-bit)
  [15]     0x00 (Reserved)

Payload:
  [16..51] Entertainment Area UUID (36 Bytes ASCII)
  [52..N]  Repeated 7-Byte Channel Blocks:
           Byte 0: Channel ID (0..19)
           Bytes 1-2: Red   (16-bit Big-Endian, 0..65535)
           Bytes 3-4: Green (16-bit Big-Endian, 0..65535)
           Bytes 5-6: Blue  (16-bit Big-Endian, 0..65535)

4. Bridge Hardware Compatibility

  • Philips Hue Bridge v2 (Square White): Fully supported. Streams up to 20 channels at 25–50 FPS.
  • Philips Hue Bridge Pro (2026 Black): Fully supported. Up to 150+ devices with built-in Wi-Fi and quad-core CPU.
  • Gradient Lightstrips: Multi-zone channels (3 zones on Ambiance, 7 zones on Play) map to the primary channel in v0.9.0-rc1, with independent spatial EQ chasers scheduled for future releases.
